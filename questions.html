NAND Forge — Playtester Quiz

Answer these once before playing and again after. Pick the single best answer for each. Every question is about building logic gates out of other gates — the skill the game trains.

1. You wire a single NAND with the same signal into both inputs: g0 = NAND(A, A). What does g0 output?
A) Always 0
B) NOT A
C) A (unchanged)
D) Always 1
2. You already have g0 = NAND(A, B). Which single added gate turns this into an AND of A and B?
A) NAND(g0, g0)
B) OR(g0, g0)
C) NAND(A, B) again
D) NAND(A, g0)
3. Building each of these from NAND gates only, which one costs the MOST NAND gates?
A) NOT
B) AND
C) OR
D) XOR
4. A student wants OR and wires g0 = NAND(A, A), g1 = NAND(B, B), then g2 = AND(g0, g1) as the output. What did they actually build?
A) NOR
B) OR (it's correct)
C) XOR
D) AND
5. In the 4-NAND XOR (g0=NAND(A,B), g1=NAND(A,g0), g2=NAND(B,g0), g3=NAND(g1,g2)), what does the output g3 produce when A=1 and B=1?
A) 0
B) 1
C) It depends on a hidden gate
D) Undefined
6. That same XOR construction reuses one gate's output by feeding it into two later gates. Which signal is reused that way?
A) The final output g3
B) g0 = NAND(A, B)
C) The raw input A
D) Nothing is reused
7. You solve a mystery truth table with 6 gates, then the "reveal minimal" shows a 4-gate version. What does that tell you about your solution?
A) Your solution produces the wrong output
B) Your solution is correct but not minimal
C) The 4-gate version must be wrong
D) 6 gates and 4 gates always behave differently
8. You're given a 2-input truth table (no gate name) that outputs 1 only for inputs 01 and 10, and 0 for 00 and 11. Which gate is this?
A) AND
B) OR
C) XOR
D) NAND
9. Trying to build NOT, a student ties the second NAND input to a constant 0: NAND(A, 0). What does this circuit output?
A) NOT A
B) Always 1
C) Always 0
D) A (unchanged)
10. Using NAND gates only, what is the minimum number needed to build NOR of two inputs?
A) 2
B) 3
C) 4
D) 5
